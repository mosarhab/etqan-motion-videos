# Asset provenance

- `public/etqan-logo.png`: exact copy of the official repository-root PNG. The display uses the previously user-authorized white-lettering/original-gold filter. Alpha, design and aspect ratio are preserved. No other laboratory or creator logo is used.
- `public/Cairo-Variable.ttf`: the repository's Cairo font. Commercial use is covered by the bundled SIL Open Font License in `Cairo-OFL.txt`.
- `public/score.m4a`: original electronic composition and synthesized Foley generated by `scripts/make_audio.py`. No third-party recordings or samples. Created for this production and available for its commercial use; no claim of exclusive registered composition rights.
- All equipment, samples, grains, water and background contours are authored SVG geometry or procedural textures in the source. No imported stock photos, generated scene stills, footage from another laboratory or 3D models.
- The cover is extracted from the final MP4. It is not used as a scene.

Software dependencies retain their upstream licenses and are pinned in `package-lock.json`. No previous user voice recordings, private uploads, spreadsheets, credentials, dependencies or render caches are included in the source archive.
