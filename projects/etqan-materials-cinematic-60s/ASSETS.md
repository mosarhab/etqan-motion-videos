# Provenance

- `public/etqan-logo.png`: unchanged copy of the original repository-root company logo, preserving design, proportions and colors.
- `public/Arabic-Medium.ttf` and `public/Arabic-SemiBold.ttf`: IBM Plex Sans Arabic from the Google Fonts repository, under SIL OFL; the unmodified license is in `public/OFL.txt`.
- `src/geography.json`: public-domain Natural Earth country polygons, retained as vector coordinates for runtime generation of the Earth material. Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Names are used internally to select Saudi Arabia; no map labels are rendered.
- `public/etqan-original-score.wav`: original synthesized instrumental and sound effects, generated by `scripts/make_audio.py`; no stock samples or third-party music track.
- All specimen and equipment geometry, particles, camera paths, surface textures, environment lighting and annotations: procedural code in `src/`.

The concept illustration images and the private monthly workbook are excluded from the project. No photography, AI still scene, external footage, or icon substitutes for equipment are used.
