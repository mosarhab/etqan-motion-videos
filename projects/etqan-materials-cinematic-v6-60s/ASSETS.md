# Asset provenance

- `public/etqan-logo.png`: byte-identical to the repository-root original (2048 × 552). `src/BrandMark.tsx` renders the user's approved white/gold variant directly from this PNG with an SVG filter. It preserves the original alpha outline and proportions and retains the gold selection from the source; it does not redraw the logo. There is no logo background panel.
- `public/Cairo-Variable.ttf`: Cairo variable typeface from https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/cairo/Cairo%5Bslnt,wght%5D.ttf . SIL Open Font License; original license in `public/Cairo-OFL.txt`. The slant is left at zero. Medium through bold weights are selected in CSS.
- `src/geography.json`: public-domain Natural Earth country polygons, retained from the earlier source as vector coordinates. https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Geometry is used to generate the globe texture and extruded Saudi map at runtime. Only Riyadh is labeled on-screen.
- `public/etqan-original-score.wav`: original synthesized instrumental and subtle effects from the previous material-led film, generated by `scripts/make_audio.py`. No stock samples or third-party music recording.
- Phone, district/city and email: supplied explicitly by the user in this conversation for the advertisement.
- All equipment, samples, soil strata, city blocks, lighting, reflection environment, textures, camera paths and particles: procedural source code. The city texture is illustrative, not survey data.

No concept image, photograph, private spreadsheet or client data is imported into the film or this project.
